Open ten AI-generated portfolios. Nine will be `from-blue-50 to-indigo-100` with a purple CTA.

It's not wrong — it's just forgettable.

## A more human palette

I rebuilt this site around **paper and ink**:

- **Paper:** `#FDFCF8` — warm, not sterile white
- **Ink:** `#1A1A18` — soft black, easier on eyes
- **Brick:** `#C45A3C` — primary, earthy confidence
- **Forest:** `#2B3D3A` — secondary, grounding
- **Ochre:** `#D9A44A` — accent, optimism without neon

Why it works: contrast is high enough for accessibility, but warmth keeps it editorial — like a well-printed book, not a dashboard.

## Rules I followed

1. **No pure gradients in hero.** Use texture, not rainbow.
2. **One accent, sparingly.** Ochre for highlights, not backgrounds.
3. **Type does the talking.** Serif headings (Newsreader) + sans body (Inter) — hierarchy without color shouting.

Try it: replace your `bg-gradient-to-br` with `bg-[#FDFCF8] border border-[#E8E0D0]` and watch the site breathe.
